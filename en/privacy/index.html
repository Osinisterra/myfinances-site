Privacy policy

In short: MyFinances does not collect your data. There are no developer servers, user accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The developer cannot see your information.

Where your data is stored

Your accounts, transactions, statements, investments and budgets are stored on your iPhone, protected by device encryption. If iCloud is on, the app syncs them to your own iCloud (CloudKit private database), which only you can read. Statement passwords, the AI key and the email session are kept in the iPhone Keychain and are not synced.

Email (optional)

If you connect Gmail or Outlook, the app requests read-only access and only looks for emails from your bank's senders (purchase and transfer alerts, statements). Those emails are read and processed on your iPhone; they are never sent to any developer server. You can disconnect email at any time from Settings or from your Google or Microsoft account security settings.

MyFinances' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

PDF statements

Statements you import (or that arrive in your email, if you allow it) are opened and read on your iPhone.

Artificial intelligence (optional, off by default)

Only if you turn it on and enter your own Anthropic key, and accept the permission prompt in the app, MyFinances sends Anthropic the text of a statement or email that no reader understands and the names of unknown merchants, to read and categorize them. Before sending, the app removes personal data: emails, ID numbers, account and card numbers, phone numbers and the account holder's name. The request goes straight from your iPhone to Anthropic with your key and is governed by Anthropic's policies.

On Android

Your accounts, transactions, statements and investments are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. There's no cloud sync; backups are a file you save wherever you choose.

Notification reading (optional). Only if you turn it on, after a notice that explains what it does, the app reads notifications from your banking apps (for example Bancolombia, Nequi, Nu, Davivienda) and text messages from a bank, to record purchases and transfers. Notifications from any other app are ignored and never stored. Everything is processed on your phone and nothing is sent to any server. You can turn it off anytime in your phone settings.

PDF statements. You open them from your phone; if they have a password, you type it to open them and it isn't saved.

Delete your data on Android: uninstall the app, or go to phone Settings → Apps → MyFinances → Storage → Clear data.

Exchange rate

To show dollars in pesos, the app fetches the official exchange rate (TRM) from datos.gov.co. That request contains none of your data.

Notifications

Alerts (card payments, budgets, statements) are created on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your iPhone. If you used iCloud, go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → MyFinances → Delete Data.

Children

MyFinances is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted here with a new date. For questions, see Support.
