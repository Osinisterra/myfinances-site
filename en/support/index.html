Frequently asked questions

Which banks are supported?

Bancolombia for now: purchase and transfer emails, plus savings and credit card statements. You can also add transactions by hand, including cash.

How do I import a statement?

Share the statement PDF with MyFinances from Files or Mail, or use the import button inside the app. If the PDF is password-protected, the app asks for it and can save it in the Keychain.

Can the app read my email without permission?

No. Only after you connect your account, with read-only access, and only your bank's emails. You can disconnect it in Settings.

How do I move my data to another iPhone?

With iCloud on, it syncs automatically. You can also go to Settings → Data → Full backup, save the file and restore it on the other iPhone.

Do I need the AI?

No. It's optional and off by default. It helps categorize transactions the app doesn't recognize.

How do I delete everything?

See “Deleting your data” in the privacy policy.
