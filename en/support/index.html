Frequently asked questions

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone, from the App Store. The Android version is on its way.

Which banks work?

Colombia's main banks: Bancolombia, Nequi, Davivienda, Nu, BBVA, Banco de Bogotá, Lulo Bank, Banco Falabella, Banco Caja Social, AV Villas and Banco de Occidente. Alerts the app already knows are added automatically; the rest stay “to review” so you can record them with one tap. The bank list updates by itself, without updating the app.

How do my transactions get in?

Four ways: your bank's emails (Gmail or Outlook), your bank's notifications (on Android), PDF statements, and by hand, including cash.

Can I connect Gmail and Outlook at the same time?

Yes. In Settings → Email connect both; the app checks each one separately and the same transaction is never duplicated.

Can the app read my email without permission?

No. Only after you connect your account, with read-only access, and only the banks' emails. You type your password on Google's or Microsoft's page, never in the app. You can disconnect it from Settings → Email.

What is “To review”?

Bank alerts or emails the app didn't fully understand. Tap them and record them by hand: the app learns that format and next time they go straight to the account you chose.

What is “Share format”?

After you record a new alert by hand, the app asks if you want to send us its format, without amounts, numbers, names or merchants, so the app recognizes it for everyone. It's optional and you can choose “Don't ask again”. Anything from Gmail is never shared.

How do I import a statement?

Share the statement PDF with MyFinances from Files or Mail, or use the import button in the app. If the PDF has a password, the app asks for it and stores it encrypted on the phone. If you allow it, it also reads the statements that arrive in your email.

How do dollar alerts work?

In Investments → Dollar today → Alerts, choose whether to be notified when the exchange rate (TRM) goes above a value, drops below another, or moves more than a percentage in a day. They're checked when you open the app and several times a day, and each alert notifies once a day.

How do I move my data to another iPhone?

With iCloud on, it syncs by itself. You can also go to Settings → Data → Full backup, save the file and restore it on the other phone.

Does the app use artificial intelligence?

Only Apple's on-device AI on your iPhone, to read statements no reader recognizes. Everything happens on the phone: nothing is sent to any server.

How do I delete everything?

See the “Delete your data” section of the privacy policy.
